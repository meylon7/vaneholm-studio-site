L01 What we do

A sales gallery opens before the building does. Our job is to make a tower feel like a place you have already stood in.

We work with developers and visualisation studios on residential towers by the water. Live action plates, a full CG building, and an edit cut to the tide.

Every film is built from the same five shots. That grammar is why a Vaneholm film reads as architecture first and advertising second.

L03 Method

Five shots. One night. The real tide.

Every plate is logged against the tide table. Water height decides the shot order on the day, so reflections in the edit always agree with the clock.

L04 Creative references

The drawing stays on the monitor.

On every shoot the elevation drawing sits next to the camera monitor. When a frame stops reading as the drawing, the camera moves until it does.
