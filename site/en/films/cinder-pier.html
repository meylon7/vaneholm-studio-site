Cinder Pier

The sharpest twist we have filmed, forty four levels turning a full quarter, shot entirely in morning fog.

L01 Project details

A brand film with the building half hidden.

Cinder Pier Estates did not want to show the tower at all. The building was still under review and the brand had to launch before it.

We filmed it in fog, so the twist appears and disappears. The film sells a shape and a mood, and leaves the detail for later.

L03 Storyline

A harbour that wakes up before the city.

The film follows the first hour of a working pier: ferries, cranes, a café shutter going up. The tower is always there, behind the fog, turning.

In the last shot the fog lifts for four seconds. That is the only time the whole building is visible.

L04 Directorial intent

Show less, so people look longer.

Fog let us hold shots for a long time without them going flat. Each frame reveals one more level than the last.

We built the CG fog volume from the real fog in the plates, measured with a light meter every ten minutes.

L05 Creative references

Harbour fog, unfinished drawings, Turner's late sea paintings.

The references were about things half seen: Turner's late harbour paintings, fog horn timetables, and the architect's early sketches with the top still missing.

We printed the elevation with the top ten levels erased and kept it on the monitor.
