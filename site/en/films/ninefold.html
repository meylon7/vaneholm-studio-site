L01 Project details

A building that only switches on at night.

Ninefold is a plain thirty level block on a disused dock. In daylight renders it looked like every other tower, so the developer asked us to find what made it different.

The answer was the night. The film is lit only by the building's own windows and the dock lamps, and follows the first nine households as their lights come on.

L03 Storyline

Nine families move in on the same evening.

The film opens on a dark block. One window lights, then another, each one a household arriving with boxes and a lamp.

By the end all nine are lit, stacked in a column that runs up the middle of the facade, and the building finally has a face.

L04 Directorial intent

Let the windows do the lighting.

We banned film lights from the exteriors. Every glow in the frame comes from a window or a dock lamp that exists in the drawings.

The CG building was lit the same way, from the inside out, so plate and render share the same sources.

L05 Creative references

Night trains, shipping containers, advent calendars.

The references were things that are dark until someone opens them: night trains passing a crossing, a stack of containers under sodium light, an advent calendar.

The drawing on the monitor marked the nine lit windows in pencil.
