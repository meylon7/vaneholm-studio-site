L01 Project details

A teaser exactly as long as the sun takes to set.

Marrow Coast needed a short piece for social channels ahead of the full launch. We proposed one idea: the teaser lasts as long as the last forty five seconds of a sunset.

The tower twists against the sun's path, so its west face catches the light floor by floor as the sun goes down.

L03 Storyline

The light climbs the building, then leaves it.

There is no character in this one. The sun is the character, moving up the facade one level at a time.

When it reaches the roof the teaser cuts to black and the name appears.

L04 Directorial intent

One real sunset, no time lapse.

We shot the plates in real time and matched the CG sun to the exact azimuth of that evening.

Nothing in the teaser is sped up. It is forty five seconds of one evening in Marseille.

L05 Creative references

Sundials, solar charts, an evening in Marseille.

We worked from the solar chart of the site and a sundial drawn onto the elevation, marking which level catches the last light at each minute.

The drawing on the monitor had the minutes written next to the floors.
