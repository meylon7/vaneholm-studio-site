L00 Studio

A film unit that thinks like an architect.

Vaneholm was founded in 2017 by director Mira Vaneholm after ten years of shooting harbours for documentaries. The first commission was a tower that existed only as a section drawing.

Today the unit works from two bases: a harbour unit in Porto for plates and a studio unit in Haifa for performance, CG and post.

L01 The five shot grammar

Every film, the same five shots.

  1. SH01 · Arrival · 24 mm

    The building first seen from the air, in the last light. It tells you where you are.

  2. SH02 · Waterline · 35 mm

    Low over the water at night. The windows arrive as reflections before they arrive as windows.

  3. SH03 · Facade · 85 mm

    A long lens up the facade. The structure, the slabs, the rhythm of the twist.

  4. SH04 · Crown · 50 mm

    The top of the building from above. The mast lamp takes over from the sun.

  5. SH05 · Sea fog · 28 mm

    Pull back into the morning fog until the building is a shape again.

L02 Method

The tide log

Every plate is logged against the tide table. Water height decides the shot order on the day, so reflections in the edit always agree with the clock.

One camera file

The CG tower and the live plates share one camera file from previs onward: lens, height, sun angle. Nothing is matched by eye in post.

The drawing on the monitor

The elevation drawing sits beside the camera monitor. A frame is right when it reads as the drawing.
